Not Equal To's

Not Equal To’s are an obsolete form of currency formerly used in the Tmgyoicunverse. They were first introduced
on July 1, 2025 at 6:50 PM, by E. 9th Street Springfield Jacksonville Florida. Originally being used in the country
of Craplandia, the currency was multipurpose, being used in everything from shops to online stores to gift money.
Not Equal To’s would eventually become obsolete in June of 2026, being fully replaced by Thumbnail Bucks.

History

In 2020, COVID-19 would strike the country of Craplandia. Since nobody was allowed to visit any stores due to the
of spreading the infection, the economy would be affected from lack of customers to physical locations. Worried
about how the economy could possibly crash, officials of the Craplandia Decisions Board would propose a new currency
called the Not Equal To, named after the math symbol. The currency could have mainly been used online, as a way to order
items. However, since this did not solve the original issue, the idea was quickly throne away, and the economy which
would indirectly lead to Craplandia’s collapse in 2026. A few years after in 2025, E. 9th Street Spingfield Jacksonville
Florida would rediscover the lost currency, before reforming it for the Tmgyoicunverse. The idea was that Not Equal To’s
could be effortlessly delivered over text conversations without Venmo or any other client. Not Equal To’s were quickly adopted,
becoming the currency for everything.

Not Equal To’s would continue to be used until June of 2026, when Thumbnail Bucks were introduced by Grant Wisler. Just
days after the currency was originally released, it was agreed that Thumbnail Bucks would be used instead of Not Equal To’s.
[m] and E9 would open an ATM allowing people to exchange their Not Equal To’s for Thumbnail Bucks, the former of which would
soon become fully obsolete.

Today, Not Equal To’s are no longer used anywhere, as Thumbnail Bucks retained the easy method of delivery, while also having
a physical image and being able to have different values, while Not Equal To’s were only worth 1≠, with no variation.
