Super Evil Systems Company

Super Evil Systems Company was a Swedish technology company that mainly existed with GammaTek
wanting to acquire Mace’s Malicious Industries. The company was formed shortly after Clarify
Corporation’s failed attempt to complete the same mission. The company was named after [m]
denied Clarify’s attempts due to them not seeming evil enough to him. Unlike Clarify, SESC
never released a single product or project, instead going straight for MMI. Super Evil Systems
Company also failed to complete the mission, and was liquidated, ending GammaTek’s attempts
to gain control of MMI.

History

After the failure of Clarify Corporation in their mission to purchase Mace’s Malicious Industries,
John Johnson decided to form a new subsidiary to try again (read the Clarify Corporation
article for more information). Since [m] originally turned them down due to them not seeming evil
enough, he gave the new subsidiary the name “Super Evil Systems Company”, giving it a red-and-black
logo, similar to that of MMI. Charlie Charlieson was appointed as the CEO of the new subsidiary. John
Johnson didn’t want to waste any time or money releasing products, so SESC was immediately sent to make
a deal with MMI. Charlie was prompted to tell Mace that Super Evil Systems Company had the same goals and
intentions as him, and that they wanted to become allies to expand eachother’s reach. Mace had Charlie
take a lie detector test, asking if SESC had any relations to GammaTek. Upon failing the test, Charlieson
was atomized by a plasma gun on one of Mace’s Genocide Men.

Shutdown

After another catastrophic failure, Super Evil Systems Company was immediately shut down, and John
decided that he would never attempt to gain control of their competitor again, after losing 2 staff
members. SESC was liquidated and absorbed into GammaTek, and after the disasters that were both Clarify
and SESC, GammaTek would end up losing over 2 million Euros, which would eventually lead to their
bankruptcy in the long-run.
