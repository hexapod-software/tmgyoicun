Clarify Corporation

Clarify Corporation was a short-lived Swedish technology company. It was formed as a subsidiary
under GammaTek Technology Corporation in August of 2026, with the intent to purchase Mace’s Malicious Industries.
Clarify would only release 2 projects, before quickly being shut down after the homicide of their lead executive, Henry Henryson.

History

In a leaked GammaTek meeting in August of 2026, Amy Amyson revealed GammaTek’s intent to acquire Mace’s Malicious Industries;
she stated that MMI had started an organization made to collapse the Global Goober Republic, which was a threat to GammaTek, as
the GGR provided their main source of currency. Amyson showed off plans to form a subsidiary under GammaTek, before making a seemingly
good deal with MMI, acquiring and shutting down the company, destroying their biggest threat. Almost immediately after this announcement,
John Johnson would officially form Clarify Corporation as a subsidiary. Charlie Charlieson was appointed as the CEO, and Henry Henryson had the
role of the lead executive.

Projects

Clarify’s first and only hardware project was a gaming monitor known as the Clarify Monitor. The Clarify Monitor featured a 4K IPS display, supporting
up to 60 Hz. Clarify stated that the monitor was designed to deliver a decent gaming monitor for a cheaper price, which is reflected by it’s $55 price tag.
However, due to lack of good marketing, as well as the fact that Clarify was a brand new company, the monitor sold very poorly, with about 20,000 units being sold.
The rest of Clarify’s unsold stock was given to Lenovo to be recycled.

Similarly. Clarify would only release one hardware product, being called GammaOS Web, a web framework for GammaTek’s scrapped GammaOS project (before it was
remade and revived under MMI). Not much is known about the framework, as the original GammaOS project never released, but it is known that it would have made
connecting to the web on GammaOS significantly easier while also offering faster connection, loading, and download speeds. The framework was fully set up inside
of GammaTek servers before GammaOS was ultimately canceled, ending development of GammaOS Web altogether.

Death of Henry Henryson and liquidation

Soon after releasing the Clarify Monitor and setting up GammaOS web, Clarify would send Henry Henryson to track down MMI CEO, [m], to try and convince him to join Clarify.
Henryson wouldn’t return for a while, and when 2 whole days passed without Henry coming back, staff members at Clarify would call the police, where they quickly
tracked down Henry’s decapitated body, which was found in a ravine. GammaTek staff members mourned the loss of Henry Henryson, realizing that this missin was a catastrophic
failure. According to sources, [m] refused the deal because their company’s logo looked too innovative and that they didn’t seem evil enough. GammaTek would quickly shut down Clarify,
bringing down the Clarify Monitor with it. The company would be fully liquidated, and the assets continued to be owned by GammaTek. However, after reflecting on the reasons as
to why [m] refused the deal, he would decide to make the risky decision to try again with the Super Evil Systems Company
