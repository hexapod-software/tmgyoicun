GammaTek Technology Corporation

GammaTek Technology Corporation, or simply GammaTek (formerly GammaTechnology and GammaTek Software),
is a technology company originally founded in Sweden in 2011. Originally founded by John Johnson, Bob Bobson,
and Alan Alanson, the company gained massive popularity for its innovative tools like GammaWeb, GammaPaint,
and Wisp, as well as hardware like the GammaPhone, GammaTablet, and GBook.
The company was formerly worth billions of dollars, before slowly being dragged down due to bad business decisions. In August
of 2026, GammaTek would file for bankruptcy before officially going defunct. However, the GammaTek trademark was soon bought by
Mace’s Malicious Industries and reformed as a subsidiary. The company continues to create software, including the new GammaOS project
and GammaTek Game Studios.

History

Founding

GammaTek traces its roots back to August of 2009, when John Johnson, a 19-year-old software engineer, wanted to move out of London
where he was born and raised. He thought that he might be able to get a better college curriculum elsewhere, while also being fed up
with the problems in the United Kingdom. He would choose to move to Sweden, as it had better healthcare, friendlier locals, and better
collages in his eyes. After moving to Stockholm and attending the Stockholm School of Economics to get a Master’s in business, he would
meet Alan Alanson. John and Alan would quickly become friends, as they had a lot in common.


Alan would eventually reveal that after previously getting a Bachelor’s in software engineering, he was attending business classes to try
to gain some of the knowledge required to start his dream technology company. After a few years of education, they would receive their
degrees and move to Ånge, where they would rent out a warehouse and a few computers. Over the course of the next 2 years, they would plan
their business together and gain some more software development knowledge. In May of 2011, another one of Alan’s friends, Bob Bobson would
join them, and in Decemeber of that same year, they would begin creating software under the name GammaTechnologies.


As GammaTechnologies, the group would develop different software experiments and post them on Reddit, hoping to gain some kind of traction.
However, they never received much attention, and all of their projects from this time-period are considered lost media. The only evidence we
have that these projects ever existed are because of a few screenshots of something labeled “PROJECT 09_a”.
